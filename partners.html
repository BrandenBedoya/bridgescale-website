How it works

Simple. Clean. Built around your existing client relationships.

  • 1 · You identify a contractor client with a capital friction problem — denied financing, declined for bonding, equipment at 30%+, can't qualify for working capital. You see this regularly — now there's somewhere to send them.
  • 2 · You submit a referral — takes 2 minutes — name, contact info, and a brief description of the issue. No paperwork. I handle everything from there.
  • 3 · I run the profile analysis within 24 hours — I contact the contractor, review their full credit and business profile, and determine whether it's a fit. You'll hear back either way.
  • 4 · You earn a referral fee when they enter the program — when the contractor enrolls, your referral fee is processed. Simple, transparent, no chasing.
  • 5 · I fix the profile. You place the business. — I repair the credit, build the business profile, and prepare the contractor for bonding and financing approval. When they're ready, I route them back to you.

What BridgeScale does

I fix the profile. You place the business.

BridgeScale Advisors is a done-for-you capital advisory program for licensed general and government contractors. I work with contractors who are technically excellent but financially invisible — and I build the infrastructure that lenders and bonding companies need to say yes.

  • Personal credit repair — legal-depth dispute strategy grounded in federal consumer protection law
  • Business credit building — D&B Paydex, Experian Business, Equifax Business
  • Entity structure review and correction for fundability
  • Bond-ready profile preparation and underwriting package assembly
  • Working capital, equipment financing, and lender matching
  • Government contracting track — SAM.gov, certifications, past performance documentation

I do not compete with referral partners. BridgeScale has no bonding capability, no lending capability, and no interest in replacing the professional relationships contractors already have. I fix the profile and route clients back to the partners who referred them.

What this is not

I know what's in the market. Here's how this is different.

  • Not a template-letter credit repair mill — the dispute strategy is grounded in FCRA, FDCPA, and CCRAA
  • Not a lender — I prepare the profile, I do not fund the loan
  • Not a competitor to referral partners — I route clients back to you by design
  • Not a quick fix — contractors who need results in 30 days are not a fit for this program
  • CROA compliant — no upfront-fee promises, full written disclosure, proper engagement agreements
  • Not a mass-market service — I work exclusively with contractors, so the process is built for their specific underwriting environment
