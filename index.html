Why contractors stall

Contractors are not blocked by skill. They are blocked by credit, bonding, and capital — in that order.

Every contractor I work with has done real work. Six-figure jobs. Solid crews. But growing from $300K remodels into $2M public work means passing through the same five locked doors, in the same order, every time. Most contractors try to skip doors. That's why they stay stuck.

  • Personal credit — without a 680+ FICO, you can't personally guarantee anything.
  • Business credit — no DUNS, no Paydex, no supplier net-30, no equipment financing.
  • Working capital — without credit: no SBA, no line of credit, no factoring approval.
  • Surety bonding — without financials and a clean indemnity profile, the underwriter declines.
  • Contract readiness — without SAM.gov, a capabilities statement, and past performance, federal work stays out of reach.

I handle all five doors in-house, in order, with one accountable team. That sequence is the product.
